Our Story – How Edge Biogene Began

Every business begins with a vision. At Edge Biogene, ours was simple: to make quality laboratory and healthcare products more accessible, reliable, and affordable for healthcare professionals.

Based in Dehradun, Uttarakhand, Edge Biogene is an emerging laboratory and medical supplies company established with a commitment to supporting the evolving needs of the healthcare industry.

1

It began with rapid diagnostics

Our journey began with a specialized focus on Rapid Diagnostic Test Cards, an essential part of modern diagnostic testing. With our understanding of diagnostic products and commitment to dependable sourcing, we started building relationships with laboratories, healthcare professionals, and institutional buyers.

2

As time passed, something important happened

Our customers began approaching us with requirements beyond rapid diagnostic products. They needed laboratory consumables, sample collection tubes, vials, syringes, medical disposables, and several other essential products used in hospitals and diagnostic laboratories.

3

We listened and expanded

Instead of limiting ourselves to a single product category, we saw an opportunity to become a more comprehensive supply partner. We listened to our customers, understood their requirements, and expanded our product portfolio to serve them better.

4

Today

What started with rapid diagnostic products gradually evolved into a broader laboratory and healthcare supply business. Today, Edge Biogene is working towards becoming a one-stop sourcing solution for hospitals, diagnostic centres, NABL-accredited laboratories, healthcare institutions, and medical supply businesses.

More Than a Supplier – Your Healthcare Sourcing Partner

At Edge Biogene, we believe every laboratory and healthcare institution has unique requirements. Some customers need regular supplies of essential laboratory consumables. Others require specific brands, specialized products, bulk quantities, or customized sourcing solutions. Our approach is designed to support both.

Through our network of manufacturers, established brands, and supply partners, we aim to provide quality products, competitive wholesale pricing, and dependable procurement support.

Because at Edge Biogene, we believe our responsibility goes beyond selling products — it is about helping healthcare businesses find the supplies they need.
