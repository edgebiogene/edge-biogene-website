Who we are

Edge Biogene is a B2B procurement and supply company based in Dehradun, Uttarakhand. We supply laboratory and hospital products on a wholesale basis to diagnostic laboratories, hospitals, clinics, research institutions and distributors.

Beyond the products we keep in stock, we source items by brand, model and specification and arrange customised bulk requirements — so buyers have one dependable point of contact for their procurement needs.
